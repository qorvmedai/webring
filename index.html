About WebRing

Engineering Industrial Solutions That Power Production

WebRing Integrated Services is a Lagos-based industrial engineering company specializing in the design, fabrication, and maintenance of food processing machinery and cosmetic production equipment.

From our workshop at Kilometer 3, Lasu-Isheri Road, Igando-Alimosho, Lagos, Nigeria. we serve manufacturers, agro-processors, and entrepreneurs across all 36 Nigeria states, 7 different countries and beyond with reliable, locally-built industrial machinery.

We also provide expert guidance on factory layout, equipment selection, production flow, and utility requirements for new production facilities.
