Industry Focus

Garri & Cassava Processing Solutions

Garri remains one of Africa's most consumed staple foods, but manual processing is inefficient, inconsistent, and limits scale. Our integrated garri processing line transforms raw cassava into finished product with minimal labour, maximum yield, and total quality control.

The Problem

Manual garri production caps output at 0.5–1 ton/day, with inconsistent quality and high labour costs.
