Industry Overview

Cassava Processing - Nigeria's Biggest Opportunity

Nigeria is the world's largest cassava producer, harvesting over 60 million tonnes annually. Cassava is processed into garri, fufu, lafun, starch, and flour - all staple foods consumed by millions of Nigerians daily. Yet a significant portion of harvested cassava is lost to spoilage due to inadequate processing capacity.

WebRing Integrated Services manufactures the complete range of cassava processing equipment - from peelers and graters to pressers, fryers, and flash dryers. Our machines help processors convert raw cassava into high-quality finished products faster, cleaner, and at higher volumes than traditional methods allow.

Production Flow

Garri Processing Workflow

01

Peeling and Washing

Remove bark and clean cassava tubers using our combined peeler-washer.

02

Grating

Grate cleaned cassava into fine mash for fermentation or immediate pressing.

03

Pressing

De-water the grated mash using our hydraulic presser to remove excess moisture.

04

Frying/Drying

Fry for garri using our automated fryer, or flash dry for cassava flour.
